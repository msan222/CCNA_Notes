#### UTP Cables (10BASE-T, 100BASE-T) Examples

- **NIC (Network interface card) on a `PC transmits data on pins 1 & 2`

- **interfaces on a `switch receive data on pins 1 & 2`

![](images/2025-11-06-16-35-27.png)

![](images/2025-11-06-16-38-37.png)

![](images/2025-11-06-16-43-16.png)

- ^Routers transmit on 1 & 2 and receive on 3 & 6

![](images/2025-11-06-16-43-53.png)

- ^called a `straight-through cable` because pin 1 on one end connects to pin 1 on the other end, etc.
    - can't use straight-through to connect like devices b/c of this

![](images/2025-11-06-16-49-20.png)

![](images/2025-11-06-16-50-30.png)

![](images/2025-11-06-16-50-54.png)

- *Routers/Firewalls/PCs all transmit on 1/2 and receive on 3/6 but Switches transmit on 3/6 and receive on 1/2

![](images/2025-11-06-16-55-38.png)

- `Auto MDI-X` - allows devices to automatically detect what pins their neighbor is transmitting on and adjust which pins it uses to send/receive
    - Common in newer equipment
